# Preparation Research and Decisions

## Product decision

- **Decision:** GO to build/evaluate the demo, conditional pilot, no production expansion approved.
- **Reason:** model benchmark exists; Routing Node/integrated demo remain to build; human/product value is unmeasured.
- **Rejected alternative:** turn a synthetic score into production GO.
- **Source:** audit and user decisions; final recommendation belongs in the memo.

## User research

- **Decision:** do not present unsupported user findings as evidence; preserve a protocol for actual collection.
- **Reason:** the product requires a declared basis for qualitative and timing claims; Assignment 3 asks for real or declared role-play conversations.
- **Rejected alternative:** anonymize invented quotes; anonymity does not make them observations.

## Scope and architecture

- **Decision:** use confirmed choices in the two `docs/design/` references.
- **Reason:** distinguish Qualifier Model from Routing Node, two human decision spaces, live Overview, dependency-scoped ambiguity, and FIFO.
- **Rejected alternative:** keep a mock-up spec as product authority.

## Brief prioritization

- **Decision:** four capability-level MUSTs; minimal workspace belongs to human decisions.
- **Reason:** course limits number of MUSTs; old feature groupings no longer describe requested experience.
- **Rejected alternative:** mark every technical component MUST in a ten-feature list.

## Planning tools

- **Decision:** reuse local Spec Kit 1.2.6.dev0 scripts/templates at repository root without overwriting nested setup.
- **Reason:** shared product requires repository-level planning; templates already exist and local resolver works.
- **Rejected alternative:** upgrade or force-reset, unnecessary and potentially destructive to adaptations.
- **Reference:** [existing-project adoption guide](https://github.com/github/spec-kit/blob/main/docs/guides/existing-projects.md).

## Unknowns and limits

No stack unknown blocks this documentary feature. Handling deadlines, critical signals, correction rights, retention, and QA rubric remain open product settings with owners. New measurements, actual human research, and unavailable course briefs are not findings of this preparatory work.
