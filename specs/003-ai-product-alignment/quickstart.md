# Alignment Validation Quickstart

Run after corrections; this is not evidence that they are complete.

## Prerequisites

- Original assignment instructions, decision register, and corrected versions.
- For sizing changes, follow the Spreadsheets skill and verify dependencies/calculation/export.
- For user research, use notes actually collected; otherwise keep that evidence open.

## Review path

1. Read README, pitch, and brief; locate the eight SC-001 decisions and check for unreported conflicts.
2. Read governance: no “read-only means zero risk,” invented confidence, or external-service exception extended to real data.
3. Review research/validation: unsupported findings are not presented as results; protocol and evidence are distinguished.
4. Reconcile memo and corrected workbook down to assumptions. Verify feasible sensitivity, cash/amortization, adoption, and per-environment costs.
5. Walk the twenty S3 cases with fixtures: intent, desk, missing/ambiguous fields, selection/clarification, and cited facts.
6. Check the requirements matrix: each supplied assignment row has a deliverable/status; unperformed research remains open.
7. Check local links, the one-baseline/one-successor version rule, and capability claims; no integrated demo build is declared functional here.

## Existing reproducible check

From repository root: `node audit/reproduce.cjs`.

Expected while historical artifacts are unchanged: 3,205 valid labels, no exact text duplicate across splits, D-q4 four silent errors of 610 and one wrong desk among 167 urgent cases. This does not validate the future Routing Node or business gains.

## Validation output

Record requirements checked, files actually corrected, limitations, missing evidence, and open recommendations in `validation.md`. Do not check off human data collection by merely reading its protocol.
