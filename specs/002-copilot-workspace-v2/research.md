# Design research

Reviewed official references on 2026-11-03. These are inspirations; no claim of using their proprietary components.

- SAP Fiori: https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-136/page-types/floorplan-overview — list/detail layout and persistent action area.
- Google Cloud: https://codelabs.developers.google.com/set-up-and-navigate-your-first-google-project — persistent global context, navigation and search.
- Databricks: https://www.databricks.com/blog/the-improved-databricks-navigation-is-enabled-for-everyone — simplify navigation around work rather than internal system structure.
- Langfuse: https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/best-practices — make provenance and execution trace inspectable, without placing trace internals in the primary task.

Decision: restrained navy chrome, white work surface, blue system information, amber human decisions, text-labelled payment status; no decorative dashboard metrics. Keep ambiguity visible rather than recommending the held payment on an unsupported inference. Show exact fixture dates and avoid inventing a date delta from “last week”.
